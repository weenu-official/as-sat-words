When my daughter A started preparing for the SAT, I did what every parent does. I bought the vocabulary books.

One had a thousand words, many of them rarely seen on today's Digital SAT, which tests words inside short passages. A was bored by page three. And every evening I found myself asking the same question: "Did you study your words today?"

So I treated it like a project. Which words actually matter? How many minutes a day are realistic for a busy student? How can I know she's on track without asking?

A's SAT Words is my answer. 350 carefully chosen words, ten original example sentences for each, short daily sessions that feel like a game, and a report I can check quietly. A started using it this fall. I'd like your child to have it too.
