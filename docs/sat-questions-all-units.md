# Digital SAT 형식 문제 검수용 목록 (350단어, 700문제)

각 문제의 **A가 정답**입니다 (앱에서는 보기 순서가 매번 섞입니다).

- **1~3유닛 (60문제):** 새로 쓴 짧은 지문
- **4~35유닛 (640문제):** 앱의 기존 예문을 그대로 쓰고 단어 자리만 빈칸으로 바꿈. 새로 만든 것은 오답 보기 3개와 해설(Why)

검수할 때 확인할 것:

1. 정답이 문장에 논리적으로 딱 맞는가
2. 오답 3개 중 정답으로도 읽힐 수 있는 것이 없는가
3. 해설(Why)이 문장의 단서를 정확히 짚는가
4. (1~3유닛) 문장이 자연스러운가

수정이 필요하면 단어와 문제 번호(예: tedious #2)로 알려주세요.

## Unit 1

### 1. unimportant

**#1** For decades, biologists treated the long stretches of DNA that do not code for proteins as ______, calling them "junk." Recent studies, however, show that many of these regions help control when genes switch on and off.

- A) unimportant ✅
- B) indispensable
- C) recognizable
- D) vigorous

> Why: Calling the DNA "junk" shows that scientists thought it had little significance; "however" signals that this view later changed.

**#2** In her notebook, the novelist recorded details that might seem ______ to anyone else: the color of a neighbor's gate, the price of bread. Years later, those small observations gave her fiction its convincing sense of place.

- A) unimportant ✅
- B) conclusive
- C) lucrative
- D) infallible

> Why: The examples are small, everyday details that most people would consider insignificant, yet they proved valuable later.

### 2. comparable

**#1** The two telescopes were built decades apart and use entirely different mirrors. Even so, tests show that the images they produce are ______ in sharpness, differing by less than one percent.

- A) comparable ✅
- B) atypical
- C) inaccessible
- D) arbitrary

> Why: "Even so" and "differing by less than one percent" show that the images are roughly equal in quality.

**#2** Economists caution against assuming that wages in the two cities are ______. Although the average salaries look similar, housing in one city costs nearly twice as much, so the same paycheck buys far less.

- A) comparable ✅
- B) tedious
- C) implicit
- D) spontaneous

> Why: The salaries only "look similar"; the warning is against treating them as truly equivalent.

- A) recognizable ✅
- B) obscure

> Why: Listeners can "identify her work within a few seconds," so the style is easy to recognize.
